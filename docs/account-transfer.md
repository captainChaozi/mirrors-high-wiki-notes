# Mirrors High account transfer (fan wiki notes)

Summary of the unofficial [account transfer guide](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/guides/account-transfer/) on [bleachmirrorshigh.lol](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/):

- Link a **BANDAI NAMCO ID** before you need it; that link is how a save moves to a new device.
- Per the official support FAQ, switching between iOS and Android loses paid Mirror Gems, gem-pack rewards and Boost Mode, with no compensation.
- The page also covers crash and loading fixes.

Check official support before acting. Maintained by a Mirrors High Wiki editor (unofficial fan wiki).
