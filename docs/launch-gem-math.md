# Mirrors High launch gems: where the "50 pulls" figure comes from (fan wiki)

Source: [Mirrors High launch rewards](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/guides/launch-rewards/) on the unofficial [Mirrors High Wiki](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/).

## Publisher-confirmed launch Gifts

Claimed from Home → Gifts / Present within 30 days of service start (2026-10-28):

- ★3 Ichigo Kurosaki [Substitute Soul Reaper]
- ★3 Rukia Kuchiki [Squad 13 Lieutenant]
- 9,000 Mirror Gems
- 5 free ★3-or-higher guaranteed summon tickets (from the 1M pre-registration release; the in-game ticket name may differ)

## Beginner tracks on top

| Track | Mirror Gems |
|---|---|
| Log in during the first 7 days | up to 3,000 |
| Clear all beginner missions | 3,000 |

## The math

9,000 + 3,000 + 3,000 = 15,000 Mirror Gems. At 300 gems per pull (see [summons](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/systems/summons/)), that is 50 pulls. Headlines that say "50 pulls" are counting all three sources, not the Gifts box alone.

## Why it matters for rerolling

The login and mission gems stack on an account you keep. Endless resetting gives some of that up; see the [reroll guide](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/guides/reroll-guide/).

These are not redeem codes. The wiki does not list invented "active" codes.
