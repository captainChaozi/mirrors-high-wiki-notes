# Mirrors High Arts affinity triangle and clashes (fan wiki)

Source: [Mirrors High combat system overview](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/systems/combat-overview/) · Wiki: [Mirrors High Wiki](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/)

Evidence note: the card types, affinity triangle and clash details come from a community transcription of the in-game help. Confirm them in the client.

## The triangle

| Arts (colour) | Strong against |
|---|---|
| Power (Red) | Blitz (Green) |
| Blitz (Green) | Projectile (Blue) |
| Projectile (Blue) | Power (Red) |

- **Same type collides into a Clash**, except Projectile vs Projectile, which cancels without a clash.
- **Advantaged Arts trigger a counter.**
- Clash win chance shifts with card affinity and a Spiritual Pressure comparison.
- Winning a clash opens a counter window with boosted damage until the combo ends.

## Where it leads

Successful card use fills the Break Attack Gauge. A Break Attack causes Guard Break. During Guard Break, actions fill the Soul Gauge for **Soul Drive**, the whole-party attack, which is stronger with more living party members.

Attacks during Guard Break may not refill the Draw Gauge, so plan your hand before breaking.

## Reading a character page with this

Each profile lists the Arts Type of Arts 1 and Arts 2 (for example Rukia: Projectile and Power). Check those two types against the enemy before choosing who stays on the field.
