## The first limited summon

Sosuke Aizen, ending **2026-11-24 05:59 UTC** (14:59 JST). Community reports say there is no free retry pool on this banner. Details: [Aizen launch banner](https://bleachmirrorshigh.lol/banners/aizen-launch-banner/).

## Launch roster

13 units with official skill pages (Ichigo, Rukia, Orihime, Chad, Uryu, Renji, Byakuya, Rangiku, Toshiro, Mayuri, Yoruichi, Grimmjow, Bambietta) plus limited Aizen. The 27 Oct broadcast also revealed 10 more Tite Kubo-designed squad members, bringing original story characters to 15.
